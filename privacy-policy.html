Privacy Policy

Health & Sport Physiotherapy Center, Jankipuram, Lucknow

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

Health & Sport Physiotherapy Center collects information you provide when requesting an appointment, calling our clinic, or filling out consultation forms. This may include your name, contact phone number, email address, preferred appointment date/time, and clinical symptoms or health notes relevant to your rehabilitation.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Schedule, confirm, and manage your physiotherapy appointments.
  • Communicate vital pre-visit guidelines or clinical updates.
  • Maintain high-standard medical records in accordance with healthcare privacy practices.
  • Improve our clinical services, clinic accessibility, and patient care.

3. Confidentiality & Data Security

Your personal and medical information is kept strictly confidential. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal health data to any third-party marketing companies. Access is restricted strictly to qualified clinical and administrative staff who require it to provide you with care.
