Getting started with us

Starting physiotherapy shouldn’t be complicated. Our team will listen to your concerns, assess your condition and build a plan that fits your goals — whether you’re an athlete, recovering from surgery or living with long-term pain.

To begin your treatment at Health & Sport Physio, follow the steps below:

  • Call us on +91 94152 88277 or visit the clinic in Jankipuram.
  • Meet your physiotherapist for a full assessment.
  • Follow your personalised treatment plan with regular reviews.
